# Image credits for /orch-or

is shown as an author of the cited work, not as an endorser of the request
for proposals. All come from Wikimedia Commons; each was downloaded as
Commons' 500 px thumbnail, cropped square to the face and resized to
200 × 200. The crops are adaptations and carry the same licence as the
original.

Entries with no usable freely licensed photo show a drawn preview of the
paper or book instead (journal or publisher and title only, no reproduced
pages): Nagel 1974, Baars 1988, Penrose 1989 (Penrose's photo is used for
1996), Tononi 2004, Casali et al. 2013, Butlin, Long et al. 2023 and Khan et
al. 2024. Commons has photos of Nagel and Tononi, but the Nagel one's
licence rests on an unverified "own work" claim for a 1978 portrait and the
Tononi one is a 113 px original, so neither is used.

The Commons page for Crick's photo dates it 2004, the date it was published in PLoS Biology, after his death; the page
does not caption it with a date.
