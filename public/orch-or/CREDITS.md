# Image credits for /orch-or

is shown as an author of the cited work, not as an endorser of the request
for proposals. All come from Wikimedia Commons; each was downloaded as
Commons' 500 px thumbnail (the originals, for Nagel and Tononi), cropped square to the face and resized to
200 × 200. The crops are adaptations and carry the same licence as the
original.

Penrose's photo is used for both 1989 and 1996. Two authors have no freely
licensed photo on Commons and show the moment's icon instead: Bernard Baars (1988) and Sana
Khan (first author, Khan et al. 2024). Nagel's photo is a 2008 classroom
shot (576 px original), not the 1978 portrait on Commons, whose licence
rests on an unverified "own work" claim; Tononi's is a 113 px original, so
it is soft even at the timeline's 56 px.

The Commons page for Crick's photo dates it 2004, the date it was published in PLoS Biology, after his death; the page
does not caption it with a date.

The same credits are listed on the page under Sources (`TIMELINE` in
`app/orch-or/data/content.ts`).
